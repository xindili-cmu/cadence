Rehab Tech and Sarcopenia Intervention: Electroacupuncture+AR Gait, and HIIT

11 updates today across 6 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Neuro] Effect of Sequential Electroacupuncture Combined With Augmented Reality Gait Training on Walking Function and Neuromuscular Control in Subacute Stroke: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Sequential electroacupuncture combined with AR gait training significantly improved gait speed (primary outcome), stride length, gait symmetry, motor function, and balance in subacute stroke patients compared to AR training alone.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation

2️⃣ [Geriatric] Effects of a multicomponent exercise programme including high-intensity interval training in institutionalised older adults with probable sarcopenia: a multicentre randomised controlled trial
This multicenter RCT clearly shows that a 12-week multicomponent exercise program including high-intensity interval training (HIIT) significantly improves handgrip strength and physical performance in institutionalized older adults with sarcopenia, with a low NNT (NNT=2) indicating substantial effect.
— BMC Geriatrics

3️⃣ [Neuro] Effects of High-Intensity Laser Therapy on Functional Outcomes and Spasticity-Related Pain in Subacute and Chronic Stroke Patients with Plantar Flexor Spasticity: A Randomized Sham-Controlled Trial.
High-intensity laser therapy (HILT) as an adjunct to stroke rehabilitation significantly and durably reduces spasticity-related pain, though its clinical significance for gait speed improvement remains uncertain.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation

4️⃣ [Neuro] Quantitative evaluation of shoulder motor compensation in stroke patients based on multimodal feature analysis.
This study's quantitative evaluation method for shoulder motor compensation, integrating markerless motion capture and sEMG, offers an objective and simplified complementary tool for assessing upper-limb function in stroke patients.
— Journal of electromyography and kinesiology : official journal of the International Society of Electrophysiological Kinesiology

5️⃣ [Sports] Isolated anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction versus reconstruction with a lateral extra-articular procedure (LET or ALL reconstruction): a systematic review and meta-analysis
For young, high-risk patients, combining ACL reconstruction with a lateral extra-articular procedure (LET or ALL) significantly reduces re-rupture rates.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders
