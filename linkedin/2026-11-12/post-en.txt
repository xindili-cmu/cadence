This systematic review clarifies that altered walking biomechanics post-ACLR are related to the interval between injury and surgery.

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Effectiveness of wearable and non-wearable biofeedback on knee biomechanics during functional tasks in anterior cruciate ligament injury prevention and rehabilitation: a systematic review and meta-analysis
Biofeedback may help reduce peak knee abduction angle in ACL injury prevention, but this is a preliminary suggestion based on low to very low certainty evidence.
— BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation
