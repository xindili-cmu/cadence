This randomized controlled trial shows that adding an 8-week neuro-athletic training (NAT) program to regular training significantly improves dynamic postural control and change-of-direction performance in soccer goalkeepers.

3️⃣ [Sports] Improvement in functional movement, landing mechanics, and dynamic balance following TRX training in physically active athletes after ACL reconstruction: a randomized controlled trial.
This randomized controlled trial clearly indicates that TRX suspension training significantly improves functional movement, landing mechanics, and dynamic balance in athletes after ACL reconstruction, outperforming traditional rehabilitation.
— BMC musculoskeletal disorders

4️⃣ [Neuro] A machine learning-based assessment method for post-stroke dysfunction severity using the ICF-RS: a prospective study

— Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation

5️⃣ [Neuro] Should Nerve Transfer Surgery be the Standard of Care for Cervical Spinal Cord Injury?
This commentary affirms the potential of nerve transfer surgery to improve upper limb function in cervical spinal cord injury patients but also highlights existing knowledge gaps, particularly regarding optimal surgical timing, rehabilitation protocols, and outcome variability.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation
