1️⃣ [Neuro] Identifying heterogeneous patterns of real-world upper limb use beyond motor severity: a cross-sectional sensor-based study in people with subacute stroke
This study used sensor data to identify six real-world upper limb use subtypes after stroke, going beyond motor severity scores.

2️⃣ [Geriatric] The effects of route previewing on adaptive gait and gaze behaviour under cognitive dual-task conditions in older adults
Route previewing effectively mitigates the negative impact of cognitive dual-task conditions on adaptive gait in older adults, reducing stepping errors and cross-steps, and improving gaze behavior.

3️⃣ [Sports] Effects of cluster-set vs. traditional resistance exercise with equated total rest on muscular performance, perceived fatigue, and cardiovascular responses in resistance-trained women
With equated total rest, cluster-set configurations significantly improve muscular endurance and reduce perceived fatigue and cardiovascular load-to-work ratios in resistance-trained women.
— Sport Sciences for Health

4️⃣ [Pediatric] Immediate effects of suspension walker support on locomotor behavior of pre-walking infants with Down syndrome
This study indicates that suspended walkers can significantly increase locomotor behavior in pre-walking infants with Down syndrome, even without prolonged practice.
