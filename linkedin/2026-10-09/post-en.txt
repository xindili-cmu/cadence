This RCT is the first to demonstrate that thoracic kinesiology taping, combined with conventional rehabilitation, significantly improves pulmonary function, inspiratory muscle strength, and functional independence in chronic post-stroke hemiplegia patients, suggesting its potential as an adjunct in neurorehabilitation.

This study demonstrates that clustering older adults based on multidimensional physical performance measures effectively identifies subgroups for frailty, fall risk, and cognitive health, highlighting muscular power and joint moments as key discriminative features.

This RCT evidence confirms that kinesio taping and myofascial release, when combined with home exercise, are effective for short-term pain relief and functional improvement in cervicogenic headache.

5️⃣ [Neuro] Relaxation interventions in neurological rehabilitation: a scoping review.
Relaxation interventions in neurorehabilitation are generally feasible and well-accepted, but evidence for their efficacy remains fragmented and largely focused on psychological outcomes.
