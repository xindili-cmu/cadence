1️⃣ [Neuro] Contralesional plantar peripheral electrical stimulation combined with contralesional posterior parietal cortex repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation promotes lower-limb motor recovery in stroke patients with hemiparesis: a randomized, double-blind, factorial controlled trial | Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation
Lower-limb motor recovery in stroke patients with hemiparesis can be synergistically promoted by combining contralesional plantar peripheral electrical stimulation with contralesional posterior parietal cortex repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation.

2️⃣ [Neuro] Effect of resistance, balance, and aerobic training on strength, mobility, balance, and quality of life in older adults with sarcopenia: a systematic review and meta-analysis | BMC Geriatrics
Combined resistance, balance, and aerobic training significantly improves strength, mobility, balance, and quality of life in older adults with sarcopenia.

3️⃣ [Sports] Delaying Early Morning Training Time in Swimmers: Effects on Sleep, Recovery and Performance—A Randomized Controlled Trial | Sports Medicine - Open
Delaying early morning training effectively improves sleep, recovery, and performance in swimmers.

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] Sarcopenia as an independent associated factor for urinary incontinence in adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis of observational studies | BMC Geriatrics
Sarcopenia is independently associated with urinary incontinence in adults, suggesting sarcopenia screening could be part of incontinence risk assessment.

5️⃣ [Sports] Temperature-dependent effects of post-exercise cold-water immersion on recovery: a systematic review and meta-analysis | BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation
The recovery effects of post-exercise cold-water immersion (CWI) are temperature-dependent.
