1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Effects of Exercise Type and Duration on Pain, Function, and Quality of Life in Patients With Knee Osteoarthritis: A Systematic Review With Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis.
This Bayesian network meta-analysis confirms that for knee OA patients, short-term (≤12 weeks) combined aerobic and resistance exercise yields the greatest improvements in pain, function, and quality of life.
— The Journal of orthopaedic and sports physical therapy

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] First-Hand Experience and the Value of Telerehabilitation for Chronic Knee Pain: A Discrete Choice Experiment Nested in a Randomized Controlled Trial.
Patients with prior telerehabilitation experience show significantly higher willingness to use and pay for it.
— The Journal of orthopaedic and sports physical therapy

3️⃣ [Orthopedic] Does low back pain improve following supervised patient education and exercise therapy targeting hip osteoarthritis symptoms? A longitudinal cohort study of 5014 patients.
This large cohort study suggests that the GLA:D® program is also effective for low back pain in hip OA patients, especially those with improved hip pain.
