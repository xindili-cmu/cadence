This meta-analysis provides strong evidence that neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES) effectively improves lower limb function and activities of daily living in post-stroke patients.

The effects of different exercise interventions after stroke vary by rehabilitation goal.

3️⃣ [Neuro] Measurement properties of observational gait analysis tools in neurological disorders: a COSMIN meta-analysis.
This COSMIN systematic review indicates that no observational gait analysis tool is fully recommended for use in neurological populations, but the Wisconsin Gait Scale (WGS) and Gait Assessment and Intervention Tool (G.A.I.T) show the best potential in stroke patients.
— Disability and rehabilitation

4️⃣ [Neuro] Balance confidence, balance, and gait capacity in chronic stroke: baseline and change correlations.
This secondary analysis of chronic stroke patients suggests a weak coupling between balance confidence and objective balance and gait capacity.
— Clinical biomechanics (Bristol, Avon)

For sports involving side-cutting maneuvers like football and basketball, ACL injury risk assessment is crucial.
