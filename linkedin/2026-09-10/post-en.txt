1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Clinical outcomes and satisfaction among low back pain patients following orthopedic manual therapy: systematic review
This systematic review affirms the clinical outcomes and satisfaction of orthopedic manual therapy for low back pain patients.
— Bulletin of Faculty of Physical Therapy

2️⃣ [Sports] Reliability, Device Agreement and Validity of Load–Velocity Profiles: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis
If you're still using load-velocity (LV) profiles for 1RM prediction, pay close attention to this systematic review's warning about inter-individual variability and device agreement.
— Sports Medicine - Open

3️⃣ [Orthopedic] Balancing Offloading and Comfort: The Effect of Forefoot Rocker Curvature on In-Shoe Plantar Pressure and Gait Kinematics
For patients at risk of diabetic foot ulceration, a straight rocker profile maximizes offloading at the metatarsal heads (mean pressure 80 kPa) but compromises ankle kinematics and comfort.

4️⃣ [Neuro] Inpatient rehabilitation after acute injury or illness for patients with and without mental health disorders: service utilization, functional improvement, and pain
							| Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine
Patients with comorbid mental health disorders after acute injury/illness require more psychologist sessions and have higher physiotherapy cancellation rates during inpatient rehabilitation, yet achieve comparable functional improvement and satisfaction.
— Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine

5️⃣ [Neuro] Evaluating the Effectiveness of an Integrated Treatment of Vestibular Rehabilitation and Cognitive Behavior Therapy for Chronic Dizziness in a Community-Based Population: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
For patients with chronic dizziness, this study suggests that a brief vestibular intervention alone is not significantly different from integrated vestibular rehabilitation with cognitive behavioral strategies (VR-CBT) in improving dizziness-related handicap and gait speed over 12 months.
— Physical therapy
