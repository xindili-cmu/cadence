1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Effect of Incorporating Perceptual Learning Into Plantar Hardness Discrimination on the Risk of Falling and Muscle Torque in Patients With Hemiplegia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
This RCT indicates that incorporating perceptual learning with plantar hardness discrimination into rehabilitation programs for hemiplegic patients significantly reduces fall risk, enhances knee extensor torque, and improves functional independence.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Comparative effectiveness of different physical therapies for axillary web syndrome after breast cancer surgery: A network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
This network meta-analysis provides comparative effectiveness evidence for physical therapies in axillary web syndrome (AWS) after breast cancer surgery.
— Physiotherapy

3️⃣ [Neuro] Predicting Robot-Based Tracking Performance After Stroke Using Lesion Features and Clinical Assessments: A Pilot Study
This pilot study suggests that clinical assessments are superior to lesion features alone in predicting robot-based tracking performance after stroke.

4️⃣ [Neuro] Implementing low-cost Capability, Opportunity Motivation and Behaviour (COM-B) interventions to increase bed-substitution rehabilitation referrals in acute stroke care.
This implementation study successfully increased bed-substitution rehabilitation referrals for acute stroke patients from 5.6% to 9.6% (p = 0.002) using COM-B model interventions.
— Disability and rehabilitation

5️⃣ [Neuro] The effects of stretching interventions in people with multiple sclerosis: a systematic review.
Although the certainty of evidence remains "very low," this systematic review suggests that stretching interventions may improve mobility, spasticity, pain, and fatigue in people with multiple sclerosis (MS).
— Disability and rehabilitation
