Video Education or HIIT Dose Response: Today's 3 Top-Scoring Studies

15 updates today across 6 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Video-based education is non-inferior to in-person education in patients undergoing arthroscopic partial meniscectomy: a non-inferiority randomized controlled trial
							| Archives of Physiotherapy
This RCT confirms the non-inferiority of preoperative video-based education for patients undergoing arthroscopic partial meniscectomy, even outperforming postoperative in-person education in terms of kinesiophobia.
— Archives of Physiotherapy

2️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Optimising Exercise Prescription: A Meta-Analysis Examining the Dose Response of Exercise Duration on Cardiorespiratory Fitness Following HIIT and MICT
HIIT is indeed superior to MICT for improving cardiorespiratory fitness.
— Sports Medicine

3️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Efficacy and safety of mitapivat in adults with transfusion-dependent α-thalassaemia or β-thalassaemia (ENERGIZE-T): a double-blind, randomised, multicentre, placebo-controlled, phase 3 trial
Mitapivat, as the first oral disease-modifying therapy, significantly reduced transfusion burden in adults with transfusion-dependent α/β-thalassaemia and was well-tolerated.
— The Lancet

4️⃣ [Sports] Perceptions and Experiences of Returning to Sport Following Myocardial Infarction: A Qualitative Study.
This qualitative study highlights the psychosocial challenges (e.g., fear and uncertainty) and support needs of patients returning to sport after myocardial infarction.
— Clinical rehabilitation

5️⃣ [Sports] When evidence fails in real life: Rethinking sports injury rehabilitation and prevention.
Traditional linear, reductionist approaches to sports injury rehab and prevention have limitations.
— Brazilian journal of physical therapy
