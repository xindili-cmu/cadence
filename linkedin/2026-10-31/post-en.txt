Kinesiophobia Post-TKA: Systematic Review on Exercise Interventions

12 updates today across 6 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Exercise-based rehabilitation interventions for kinesiophobia after total knee arthroplasty: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
This systematic review found that exercise-based rehabilitation significantly reduced kinesiophobia (SMD -1.22) and improved knee function (SMD 1.19) after total knee arthroplasty (TKA).
— BMC musculoskeletal disorders

2️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Prone positioning and high-frequency oscillatory ventilation in early paediatric acute respiratory distress syndrome (PROSpect): an international, two-by-two factorial, response-adaptive, phase 3, randomised controlled trial
The PROSpect trial provides high-level evidence that prone positioning and HFOV are not recommended as first-line strategies for early, moderate-to-severe PARDS, directly impacting clinical guidelines and practice for pediatric critical care.
— The Lancet

3️⃣ [Neuro] Upper Limb Neurorehabilitation Using a Hybrid Technology-Enabled Therapy Approach-A Scoping Review.
This review indicates that hybrid technology (e.g., robotics, VR, sensor-based) for upper limb neurorehabilitation in stroke patients shows positive outcomes and feasibility in hospital settings.
— NeuroRehabilitation

4️⃣ [Neuro] Identifying key components for psychosocial interventions in neurorehabilitation: a realist evaluation on young adults with an acquired brain injury.
For psychosocial interventions in young adults with acquired brain injury (including stroke), this study suggests three pillars: individual engagement, community empowerment, and equipping professionals.
— Disability and rehabilitation

5️⃣ [Neuro] Motor memory consolidation during sleep after stroke: the rehabilitation timing hypothesis.
This review introduces the "rehabilitation timing hypothesis," suggesting that scheduling post-stroke motor rehabilitation close to sleep may enhance recovery by optimizing motor memory consolidation during sleep.
— Topics in stroke rehabilitation
