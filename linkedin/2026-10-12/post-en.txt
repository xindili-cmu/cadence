New Advances in Knee Osteoarthritis Treatment and Frailty Management

13 updates today across 4 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Blood flow restriction with different loads versus moderate intensity resistive exercises in patients with knee osteoarthritis: a single-blind randomized controlled trial
Consider passive blood flow restriction at 70% limb occlusion pressure for knee OA patients who cannot tolerate conventional moderate-intensity resistance training.
— BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Trajectories of physical activity following digital education and exercise therapy—a longitudinal registry study among 15,197 persons with knee or hip osteoarthritis
This large-sample longitudinal study shows that digital education and exercise therapy can effectively increase physical activity levels in knee/hip osteoarthritis patients, with nearly 60% reaching or maintaining recommended activity levels.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

3️⃣ [Orthopedic] Exercise prioritization in frailty: a pathophysiology-informed narrative review for rehabilitation in older adults
This review proposes a pathophysiology-informed prioritization framework for exercise interventions in frail older adults.
— European Review of Aging and Physical Activity

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] Physiotherapists' perspectives on direct access in a referral-based system in Austria - A qualitative study.
In referral-based systems like Austria, physiotherapists feel competent for direct access but need formal legal frameworks and red-flag screening training.
— Clinical rehabilitation

5️⃣ [Neuro] A normative torque-profile approach for quantifying elbow and wrist flexor hyper-resistance after stroke: a proof-of-principle study
This proof-of-principle study introduces ScoreE, a novel isokinetic dynamometry-derived metric for objectively quantifying post-stroke elbow and wrist flexor hyper-resistance.
