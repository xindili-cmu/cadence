VR/AR Robotic Gait Training and Paretic Limb Kinematics in Stroke

5 updates today across 3 specialties. 4 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Neuro] Virtual and Augmented Reality Feedback During Robotic Gait Training Acutely Modulate Paretic Muscle Activation, Limb Loading, and Propulsion-Related Mechanics in Chronic Hemiparetic Stroke: A Randomised Crossover Trial.
This randomized crossover trial shows that virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) feedback acutely and differentially modulate paretic gait mechanics in robot-assisted gait training for chronic hemiparetic stroke, with combined use yielding the broadest effects.

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Integrated care for chronic musculoskeletal (MSK) disorders is promising but incomplete: a scoping review of MSK service-delivery approaches across systems
PTs are heavily involved (67%) in integrated care for chronic MSK conditions, yet true whole-system integration and caregiver involvement remain limited.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

3️⃣ [Neuro] Venous flow dynamics in post-stroke hemiparetic lower extremity motor dysfunctions.
Stroke hemiparetic limbs exhibit significant venous flow impairment, linked to impaired calf muscle pump function, and potentially associated with motor recovery and functional status.
— Topics in stroke rehabilitation

While Japanese neurological physical therapists widely perceive instrumented gait assessment as necessary, its routine use remains limited.
