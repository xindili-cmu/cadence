ACL Reconstruction: Shear Wave Elastography for Lower Limb Muscle Stiffness

9 updates today across 5 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Sports] Shear wave elastography for assessing lower extremity muscle stiffness after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction: A systematic review.
SWE reveals reduced passive shear modulus and stiffness in the vastus medialis and semitendinosus after ACLR (g values between -1.10 and -1.64), suggesting that even patients meeting strength benchmarks may still have higher than expected landing stiffness and re-injury risk.
— Clinical biomechanics (Bristol, Avon)

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Impact of Daily Physical Fatigue on Inter-Limb Load Asymmetry and Joint Loading Rates During Daily Living Tasks in Unilateral Knee Osteoarthritis
This study found that physical fatigue exacerbates inter-limb load asymmetry in unilateral knee OA patients during daily tasks, especially stair negotiation.
