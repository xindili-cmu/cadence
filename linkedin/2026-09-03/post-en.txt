1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Sensorimotor effects of heatwrap and exercise in acute low back pain: results of a randomised controlled trial
This RCT confirms the benefits of heatwrap combined with exercise for acute low back pain in reducing pain sensitivity, improving lumbar muscle activity, and increasing trunk flexion range of motion.

2️⃣ [Practice] Home-based digital exercise versus hospital physiotherapy for plantar fasciitis: a propensity-score-matched retrospective cohort study with dual-perspective cost-utility evaluation.
This study demonstrates that home-based digital exercise is non-inferior to hospital physiotherapy for plantar fasciitis in both clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness.
