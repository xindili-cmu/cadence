Two High-Score Systematic Reviews: Somatosensory Interventions for Stroke and Percutaneous Electrolysis for Musculoskeletal Disorders

7 updates today across 2 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Percutaneous Electrolysis for the Management of Musculoskeletal Disorders. A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis.
This systematic review on percutaneous electrolysis (PNE) for musculoskeletal disorders (myofascial pain syndrome and tendinopathy) indicates that when added to therapeutic exercise, PNE is not superior to sham or exercise alone.
— Physiotherapy research international : the journal for researchers and clinicians in physical therapy

2️⃣ [Neuro] Effects of Somatosensory Interventions on Sensory, Motor, and Balance Functions in Individuals with Stroke: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Somatosensory interventions show a significant improvement in balance function for stroke patients.
— NeuroRehabilitation

3️⃣ [Neuro] Association Between Admission Serum Albumin and Short-Term Functional Improvement During Inpatient Rehabilitation After Cervical Traumatic Spinal Cord Injury: A Retrospective Cohort Study.
This retrospective study suggests that higher serum albumin levels at admission for cervical traumatic spinal cord injury are associated with greater short-term functional improvement in basic ADL.
— NeuroRehabilitation

4️⃣ [Neuro] Priorities and design principles for a mobile health (mHealth) application to support self-management of post-stroke aphasia: experience gathering and prioritisation within an experience-based co-design approach.
This study, co-designed with 13 people with aphasia, 8 significant others, and 10 health professionals, identified five key design principles for mHealth apps supporting post-stroke aphasia self-management.
— Disability and rehabilitation

5️⃣ [Neuro] A sex-positive approach to sexual rehabilitation after SCI: exploring women's sexual experiences using participatory action research.
This study of 15 women with spinal cord injury (SCI) suggests that a sex-positive approach is essential when addressing sexual health in SCI rehabilitation.
— Disability and rehabilitation
