1️⃣ [Geriatric] Effectiveness of different exercise types for improving lumbar spine and femoral neck bone mineral density in older adults: a three-level pairwise and Bayesian network meta-analysis
For older patients, exercise remains the cornerstone for improving lumbar spine and femoral neck BMD; this high-quality systematic review confirms it.
— BMC Geriatrics

2️⃣ [Neuro] Virtual Reality and Body Weight-Supported Treadmill Training in Gait and Balance Rehabilitation After Stroke: A Systematic Review of Randomized Controlled Trials.
Both Virtual Reality (VR) and Body Weight-Supported Treadmill Training (BWSTT) can serve as adjuncts for gait and balance rehabilitation after stroke, with effects potentially comparable to conventional rehabilitation.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation

3️⃣ [Geriatric] Multisite Implementation and Outcomes of KineTransForma: A Faculty-Supervised, Student-Delivered, Community-Based, Goal-Directed Home Rehabilitation Program in Chile.
This pragmatic program evaluation from Chile indicates that a student-delivered, faculty-supervised community-based home rehabilitation model can effectively improve outcomes for older adults with functional dependency.
— Physical therapy

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] Referral for knee pain and gait training leads to multiple sclerosis diagnosis: a case report.
This case report reminds you to remain vigilant and investigate symptoms not fully explained by typical musculoskeletal findings, even when a plausible diagnosis exists.
— The Journal of manual & manipulative therapy

5️⃣ [Neuro] Low-Cost Visual Art in Stroke Neurorehabilitation: A Scoping Review.
Low-cost visual art interventions may serve as adjuncts in stroke rehabilitation, particularly for improving communication, mood, self-efficacy, and fine motor outcomes, though further research is needed to clarify neuroplasticity links and implementation guidelines.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation
