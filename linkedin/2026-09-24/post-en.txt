Supervised Exercise in Pregnancy & Exercise Prescription for Breast Cancer: Two Top-Scoring Systematic Reviews Today

16 updates today across 6 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Effects of supervised exercise during pregnancy on gestational diabetes mellitus and other pregnancy outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis
Supervised exercise during pregnancy significantly reduces the risk of gestational diabetes, gestational hypertension, excessive gestational weight gain, and macrosomia.
— British Journal of Sports Medicine

2️⃣ [Sports] Optimising exercise prescription for cancer-related fatigue, lean mass, physical function and muscle strength in women with breast cancer: a systematic review with dose-response network meta-analysis
For breast cancer patients, combined aerobic and resistance exercise is most effective for fatigue and lean mass, while resistance exercise is best for physical function and strength.
— British Journal of Sports Medicine

3️⃣ [Neuro] Effects of TENS, NMES, and Combination Therapy on Hemiplegic Shoulder Pain and Function After Stroke.
For early stroke rehabilitation, integrated electrical stimulation may be more effective than unimodal approaches for hemiplegic shoulder pain and function.
— Archives of physical medicine and rehabilitation

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] Effects of Using High-Frequency TENS During an Exercise Therapy Program in Knee Osteoarthritis: A Randomized Sham-Controlled Trial.
High-frequency TENS does not significantly augment the benefits of exercise therapy for knee osteoarthritis.
— Physiotherapy research international : the journal for researchers and clinicians in physical therapy

5️⃣ [Neuro] Machine learning-based identification of a minimal wearable sensor for gait assessment in vestibular schwannoma
This preliminary study suggests that a wrist-worn sensor during eyes-closed gait tasks can achieve 60-83% accuracy in assessing gait for vestibular schwannoma patients.
— Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation
