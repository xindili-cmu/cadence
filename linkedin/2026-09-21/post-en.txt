2️⃣ [Sports] Single-bundle versus double-bundle reconstruction in anterior cruciate ligament: a meta-analysis
Regarding ACL reconstruction, this meta-analysis on single-bundle (SB) versus double-bundle (DB) techniques found that DB statistically improved specific objective stability parameters and long-term Lysholm scores, but without clinical meaningfulness for most patients.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders
