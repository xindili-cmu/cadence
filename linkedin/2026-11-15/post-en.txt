1️⃣ [Geriatric] Gait variability as a predictor of fall risk in patients with stroke: A 6-month prospective cohort study with survival analysis.
This prospective cohort study indicates that stride time variability (STV) at discharge is an independent predictor of fall risk within 6 months post-discharge for stroke patients, with a 3.40% cutoff value aiding early risk identification.
— Gait & posture

2️⃣ [Manual & Modality] Ultrasound-guided versus landmark-guided injections in musculoskeletal practice: a scoping review of clinical efficacy, educational needs, and economic considerations
This scoping review indicates that ultrasound-guided injections reliably improve placement accuracy and may offer short-term symptomatic relief at selected sites, but long-term clinical superiority and cost-effectiveness are not established.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

In high-performance sport support systems, you need to integrate established decision principles with qualitative triage, rather than relying on a single model.

Routine physical function assessments (e.g., gait speed, grip strength, and frailty) are not only about physical health but may also predict cognitive function.
