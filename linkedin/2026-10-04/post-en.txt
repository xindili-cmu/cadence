1️⃣ [Geriatric] Transitions and predictive factors of cognitive frailty states in rural older adults: a prospective cohort study
This large cohort study is the first to clearly establish the reversible nature of cognitive frailty in rural older adults and identifies the early RCF state as a critical intervention window.
— BMC Geriatrics

2️⃣ [Neuro] An exploration of self-evaluation of induced temporal and spatial gait asymmetry in neurotypical young adults.
This preliminary study in neurotypical adults suggests that in post-stroke gait asymmetry rehabilitation, patients' self-evaluation of temporal asymmetry may be more accurate than spatial asymmetry, and focusing attention on the swing phase could improve assessment accuracy.
— Gait & posture

3️⃣ [Sports] OTC analgesic use was associated with a higher rate of return to unrestricted play (aHR 1.
This observational study suggests that OTC analgesics (especially acetaminophen) are associated with improved concussion symptoms and faster recovery.
— British Journal of Sports Medicine

4️⃣ [Geriatric] How to ensure the implementability of multi-component interventions to improve active ageing in older people? An overview of the evidence of systematic reviews
This overview of reviews highlights a gap between the use of implementation frameworks and the reporting of implementation outcomes in multi-component interventions for active aging.
