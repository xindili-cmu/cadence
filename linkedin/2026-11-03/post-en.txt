Generative AI, especially LLMs, shows significant potential in rehabilitation treatment planning, but must address "hallucinations" and undergo rigorous real-world validation before clinical integration.
— American journal of physical medicine & rehabilitation

3️⃣ [Neuro] A Retrospective Real-World Assessment of Motor Status After Stroke Using a Home-Based Brain-Computer Interface
This real-world retrospective study suggests that at-home brain-computer interface (BCI) therapy provides sustained motor improvements for stroke survivors in routine care, particularly those with severe chronic impairment.

4️⃣ [Pediatric] Effectiveness of Plyometric Exercises Versus Flatfoot Corrective Exercises on Balance and Foot Posture in Obese Children with a Flexible Flatfoot: A Comparative Study
This study indicates that plyometric exercises are superior to traditional corrective exercises for improving balance in obese children with flexible flatfoot, which is an important consideration when selecting interventions for pediatric foot posture and gait training.

5️⃣ [Sports] From Signal to Biological Meaning: A Systematic Review of Construct-Validity Evidence for External Training Load Metrics in Sport
This systematic review indicates that most external training load (ETL) metrics primarily describe external movement and work, rather than direct biological demands or responses.
— Sports Medicine - Open
