Structured Exercise and Cancer Survival: Systematic Review Provides Evidence

21 updates today across 7 specialties. 4 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Structured exercise interventions and survival outcomes in patients with cancer: systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials
Structured exercise interventions not only improve quality of life and physical function in cancer patients but also significantly prolong overall survival (HR 0.77) and disease-free survival (HR 0.83).
— British Journal of Sports Medicine

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Optimal exercise dose in adults with chronic low back pain disorders: systematic review with dose-response network meta-analysis.
This systematic review of 239 trials provides exercise dose recommendations for chronic low back pain, but emphasizes very low certainty of evidence.
— British journal of sports medicine

3️⃣ [Orthopedic] Combining three-dimensional ankle exercises with short-foot training improves proprioception and dynamic balance in female karatekas with flatfoot: A randomized controlled trial.
For flatfoot karate athletes, this RCT suggests that combining three-dimensional ankle exercises with short-foot training is superior to short-foot training alone for improving foot posture, proprioception, and dynamic balance.
— Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine

4️⃣ [Neuro] Opioid use following spinal cord injury: a qualitative study of the perspectives of healthcare providers and people with lived experience.
This qualitative study highlights the normalization of opioid use in both inpatient and community settings after spinal cord injury, and challenges within the Australian healthcare system for comprehensive pain management.
— Disability and rehabilitation
