8 updates today across 6 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Sports] Comparison of Radiofrequency Microtenotomy vs. Heavy-Slow Resistance for Refractory Plantar Fasciopathy: A Randomised Controlled Trial
For refractory plantar fasciopathy, this RCT clearly indicates that radiofrequency microtenotomy is not superior to heavy-slow resistance training.
— Sports Medicine - Open

2️⃣ [Pediatric] Physical and psychosocial outcomes in children with limb loss 2-years after the 2023 Kahramanmaras-Türkiye earthquakes
Two years post-earthquake, children with limb loss showed favorable physical, social, and school-related quality of life, but emotional well-being remained affected.

3️⃣ [Manual & Modality] Radiographic quantification of active self-correction during physiotherapeutic scoliosis-specific exercises and its potential prognostic value in adolescent idiopathic scoliosis
This prospective study for the first time radiographically quantified active self-correction (ASC) during PSSE in adolescent idiopathic scoliosis and found it correlated with Cobb angle change at 6 months.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] The effect of multi-joint lower-limb training on lower-limb function after total knee arthroplasty: a randomised controlled clinical trial
This study suggests that adding hip-ankle training to conventional rehabilitation after TKA modestly improves early postoperative LEFS scores at 4 weeks, but the clinical significance is limited and the benefit is not sustained.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

5️⃣ [Geriatric] GLIM-defined malnutrition and frailty in older outpatients with diabetes
This observational study reveals that GLIM-defined malnutrition is highly prevalent (33%) and independently associated with frailty in older outpatients with diabetes.
— Aging Clinical and Experimental Research
