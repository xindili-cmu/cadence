Post-COVID Condition: Systematic Review Updates Efficacy of Physical, Psychological, and Alternative Interventions

6 updates today across 5 specialties. 5 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Sports] A multimodal non-fatiguing recovery strategy improves neuromuscular restoration and attenuates inflammatory and oxidative stress responses following a standardized football match: a randomized crossover study
This study indicates that a multimodal non-fatiguing recovery strategy (e.g., very low-intensity cycling, foam rolling, and percussive therapy) may better support short-term neuromuscular recovery and attenuate biochemical disturbances after a football match compared to traditional active running or passive recovery.
— BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation

2️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Effectiveness of physical, psychological, and alternative interventions for post-COVID-19 condition: a systematic review
							| Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine
This systematic review found significant benefits of supervised cardiopulmonary rehabilitation and multimodal exercise for improving cardiorespiratory fitness and walking capacity in post-COVID-19 patients, with Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) also effective in reducing fatigue and concentration issues.
— Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine

3️⃣ [Sports] What’s Behind the Label? Rethinking Exercise Prescription for Tendinopathy - BJSM blog - social media's leading SEM voice
This commentary emphasizes that in tendinopathy rehabilitation, you should not just record general exercise names like "eccentric, isometric, or heavy slow resistance," but rather detailed "mechanical doses" such as actual load, repetitions, speed, range, and patient response.
— British Journal of Sports Medicine

4️⃣ [Pediatric] Development of a Communication Characteristics Module for Autistic Adults and the Creation of a Communication Profile Variable
We now have a validated 10-item Communication Characteristics module and a five-level Communication Profile variable for assessing communication diversity in autistic adults.

5️⃣ [Geriatric] Fall prevention interventions among older adults in low-and middle-income countries (LMICs): an integrative review.
In low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), multifactorial interventions, exercise, and home safety modifications can reduce fall incidence, fall risk, and fear of falling among older adults.
— Disability and rehabilitation
