1️⃣ [Neuro] Impact of Adding Fatigue-Focused Cognitive Behavior Therapy to Supervised Pilates-Based Core Stability Training on Balance and Walking in Patients with Multiple Sclerosis: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Fatigue-focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) combined with Pilates-based core stability training significantly improves balance and gait in multiple sclerosis patients, outperforming Pilates alone.
— Clinical rehabilitation

2️⃣ [Neuro] Self-guided home-based rehabilitation for upper limb motor recovery in people with stroke: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
For stroke patients, self-guided home-based upper limb rehabilitation is comparable to conventional therapy, making it a viable and accessible alternative.
— Disability and rehabilitation

3️⃣ [Neuro] Impact of Rehabilitation Initiation Timing on Safety and Efficacy in Patients With Acute Stroke and Concurrent Heart Failure.
Early rehabilitation (on admission day or day 1) in stroke patients with concurrent heart failure is associated with lower in-hospital respiratory failure risk, 90-day stroke readmission rates, and higher discharge Barthel Index.
— Neurorehabilitation and neural repair

4️⃣ [Pediatric] Application of the ICF in the functioning assessment of children with suspected developmental coordination difficulties.
Children with probable Developmental Coordination Disorder (p-DCD) not only have motor skill limitations but also significantly lower community participation.
— Brazilian journal of physical therapy

5️⃣ [Manual & Modality] Manual techniques for headache: who delivers what and where? A systematic review of randomized controlled trials with a practice-mapping analysis.
This systematic review found that manual techniques are widely used in headache management, but specific technique selection is more influenced by professional background, institutional setting, and regional practice traditions, rather than standardized, diagnosis-driven approaches.
— The Journal of manual & manipulative therapy
