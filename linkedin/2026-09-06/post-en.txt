This study indicates that finger-worn accelerometers are a valid and sensitive alternative to wrist-worn accelerometers for measuring real-world upper-limb performance in stroke survivors.

3️⃣ [Manual & Modality] Manual Lymph Drainage in Lower Extremity Lymphedema Management: An Essential Component or An Optional Extra? A Single-Blind, Randomized Non-Inferiority Trial
This non-inferiority trial suggests that, during the intensive phase of lower extremity lymphedema treatment, compression bandaging alone may be non-inferior to combined manual lymphatic drainage for reducing limb volume and improving tissue stiffness.
