1️⃣ [Neuro] A mobile-based multimodal monitoring system with clinician feedback for home rehabilitation after stroke: a randomized clinical trial
This RCT demonstrates that integrating multimodal monitoring with clinician feedback significantly improves walking endurance in post-stroke home rehabilitation.
— Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation

2️⃣ [Orthopedic] Pelvic Floor Yoga for Women with Overactive Bladder-Wet: A Subgroup Analysis of a Multisite Randomized Trial
For women with OAB-wet, pelvic floor yoga led to greater reductions in urinary distress and daytime voiding frequency compared to general physical conditioning, indicating its potential as a community-based rehabilitation approach.
— International Urogynecology Journal

3️⃣ [Neuro] Impact of age and frailty on the effect of a multimodal individualized long-term intervention after stroke: the last-long trial
							| Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine
The LAST-long trial indicates that multimodal stroke interventions may be less effective, and potentially even detrimental, for stroke patients aged 80 and older.

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] Anatomical Variations and Morphometry of the Pudendal Nerve: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
This meta-analysis highlights the significant anatomical variability of the pudendal nerve, providing data to enhance precision and safety during pelvic surgeries or anesthesia.
— International Urogynecology Journal

5️⃣ [Neuro] From simulation to stimulation: a predictive synergy-based control strategy for functional electrical stimulation cycling in a person with spinal cord injury
This preliminary study validates the feasibility of a predictive synergy-based control strategy for functional electrical stimulation (FES) cycling in individuals with spinal cord injury.
