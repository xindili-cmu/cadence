This retrospective study suggests that Geriatric Interdisciplinary Intermediate Care (GIMC) may be associated with lower immobility and fewer recurrent falls in older adults after hip fracture.
— BMC Geriatrics

This systematic review highlights that current evidence on whether lumbar strengthening exercises effectively reduce kinesiophobia in chronic low back pain patients is very limited and of low quality.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders
