Evidence Update: Pelvic Floor Dysfunction in CrossFit Women and Electrical Stimulation in Sarcopenic Older Women

3 updates today across 3 specialties. 3 worth your time 👇

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Prevalence and Associated Factors of Pelvic Floor Dysfunction in Women Who Practice CrossFit®: A Systematic Review
This systematic review highlights the high prevalence of pelvic floor dysfunction, particularly urinary incontinence, among women who practice CrossFit.
— International Urogynecology Journal

2️⃣ [Geriatric] Does whole-body electromyostimulation improve functional, compositional, and biochemical outcomes in older women with sarcopenia? A randomized controlled trial
This RCT suggests that whole-body electromyostimulation (WB-EMS) can improve physical function, body composition, and myokine levels in sarcopenic older women.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

3️⃣ [Sports] Strength Training in Youth: A Scoping Review with Evidence Gap Map
This scoping review found that youth strength training research predominantly focuses on physical performance, with significant gaps in female representation, biological maturation assessment, long-term follow-up, and psychosocial outcomes.
— Sports Medicine - Open
