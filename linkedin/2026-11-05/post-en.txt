1️⃣ [Neuro] Wearable-based digital metrics reveal dynamic dissociations between upper limb capacity and real-world use over the first six months after stroke
This study emphasizes that upper limb "capacity" and "real-world use" are distinct targets in stroke recovery.

2️⃣ [Geriatric] Adding blood flow restriction to low-load resistance training for sarcopenia and cognitive function in oldest-old adults: an exploratory randomized controlled trial
This exploratory RCT suggests that low-load blood flow restriction resistance training (BFR-RT) is safe and feasible for the oldest-old adults with sarcopenia and mild cognitive impairment.
— BMC Geriatrics

3️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Effects of chair-based exercise training on pulmonary function, exercise capacity, and quality of life in patients with acute exacerbation of COPD: A randomised controlled trial.
This study provides strong evidence for implementing chair-based exercise training during hospitalization for acute COPD exacerbations.
— Physiotherapy

4️⃣ [Orthopedic] Postoperative rehabilitation after medial meniscus posterior root repair: a scoping review of published protocol descriptions and evidence gaps
This scoping review reveals that rehabilitation protocols after medial meniscus posterior root repair lack consistent descriptions for active and functional training.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

5️⃣ [Neuro] Effects of low-dose cervical paraspinal lidocaine injections on cervicogenic dizziness-related outcomes: a randomized, sham-controlled, assessor-blinded trial.
Low-dose cervical paraspinal lidocaine injections show a modest short-term improvement in dizziness-related disability for cervicogenic dizziness, but clinical significance is limited and it has not been tested with rehabilitation.
— Disability and rehabilitation
