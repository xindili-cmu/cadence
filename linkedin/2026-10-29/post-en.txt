1️⃣ [Sports] The effectiveness of proprioceptive training for the prevention of ankle sprains in athletes: a systematic review and meta-analysis
This systematic review and meta-analysis provides strong evidence that proprioceptive training reduces the relative occurrence of ankle injury in athletes by approximately 34%.
— BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders

2️⃣ [Neuro] A pragmatic, decentralized trial of the home-based, autonomous InTandem® neurorehabilitation system: analyses of engagement, safety, and effectiveness from the OrcHESTRAS trial
The autonomous, home-based InTandem® neurorehabilitation system achieved high engagement (81.9%) and significantly improved 6MWT distance and TUG time in chronic stroke survivors.
— Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation

3️⃣ [Orthopedic] Effects of 12-week massage on pain, muscle co-contraction, and knee loading in medial knee osteoarthritis: a randomized clinical trial
This RCT of 56 medial knee OA patients shows 12 weeks of massage therapy significantly improves WOMAC pain, stiffness, and physical function, and reduces medial muscle co-contraction.
— Aging Clinical and Experimental Research

4️⃣ [Geriatric] Individual variability and predictors of responsiveness in physical outcomes following multicomponent physical training in older adults
This study in 314 older adults reveals significant variability in responses to a 12-month physical training program, with 46% showing a high response across multiple outcomes.
— European Journal of Applied Physiology

5️⃣ [Orthopedic] What are the barriers and facilitators to engagement with physiotherapy among women experiencing adverse effects associated with menopause?
This qualitative study highlights the importance of direct inquiry, individualized care plans, and health education in improving adherence to physiotherapy among perimenopausal women with MSK pain.
— Physiotherapy
