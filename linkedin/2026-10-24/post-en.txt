Foot Exercise Impact on Movement Control Post-ACL Reconstruction: An Exploratory RCT

1️⃣ [Orthopedic] Comparison of short-foot and toe-curl exercises on movement control during single-leg landing after ACL reconstruction: An exploratory randomized controlled trial.
For ACLR rehabilitation, Short-Foot Exercise (SFE) is superior to Toe-Curl Exercise (TCE) in improving frontal-plane movement control, with a substantial effect size (ES -1.41).
— Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine

2️⃣ [Neuro] Construct validity and intrasession intra-rater reliability of the stair and step tests in individuals with multiple sclerosis.
For individuals with Multiple Sclerosis (MS), the Stair Test and Step Test demonstrate excellent reliability, measurement precision, and construct validity.
— Physiotherapy theory and practice

3️⃣ [Neuro] Single-Case Design for Network-based BCI Rehabilitation: Individual Learning Trajectories and Effects of Motor Imagery Timing in Stroke Patients
This study indicates highly individualized outcomes in stroke patients undergoing brain-computer interface (BCI) training, with no significant impact of motor imagery (MI) decoding window timing on BCI accuracy, fatigue, or spasticity.

4️⃣ [Geriatric] Impact of malnutrition and protein-enriched oral nutrition on early recovery after fragility fractures in older adults: the NuTra trial
This RCT shows that protein-enriched diets for older adults with fragility fractures at nutritional risk during acute hospitalization did not yield significant short-term benefits in complications or functional recovery.
— BMC Geriatrics

5️⃣ [Cardiopulmonary] Validation and prognostic value of the five-repetition sit-to-stand test in patients with severe exacerbation of COPD.
The five-repetition sit-to-stand (5STS) test can assess muscle strength and exercise tolerance in hospitalized patients with severe COPD exacerbation and offers acceptable prognostic value for 6-month readmission risk.
— Brazilian journal of physical therapy
